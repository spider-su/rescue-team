# Rescue Team — Google Play listing

Language: en-US

## Title

Rescue Team

## Short description

Team up specialist heroes to solve rescue puzzles and bring civilians to safety

## Full description

Every rescue begins with a plan. Guide a crew of specialists through handcrafted grid puzzles, clear a safe route and carry civilians to the exit.

Three specialists, one team
Maya the firefighter clears fire. Theo the engineer pushes crates onto switches, operates valves and repairs bridges. Nia the containment ranger stuns infected blockers to open a path for the crew. Choose who acts next and combine their abilities in the right order.

Handcrafted missions
Play 20 campaign missions across the Coastal District and Old Town Quarantine. Three optional Floodworks trials add water, valves and bridge puzzles after your early rescues.

Room to think
Tap a hero or portrait to select them, then tap a reachable destination. There is no countdown. Undo a destination tap, restart a mission or use layered hints when you need help. Resume an unfinished rescue with its saved actions.

Bring life back to the district
Earn stars, improve your best move counts and unlock the next mission. Saved rescues restore district addresses and unlock cosmetic crew insignia that leave puzzle rules unchanged.

Play offline
Campaign progress, unfinished missions, crew choices and sound preferences are saved locally. Original illustrated locations, music and rescue effects bring the crew's work to life. Music and sound can be switched independently.

No account is needed to play. Plan carefully, work together and find a way home.
