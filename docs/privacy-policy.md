# Rescue Team privacy policy

**Status:** Publication draft — publisher/contact confirmed; legal review, effective date and release-build verification pending.

**Effective date:** [To be set at publication]

Rescue Team is an offline puzzle game published by **Alex Kotik**, acting as an individual and not under a JDG business identity. The public Google Play developer name is **smart-box**. This policy describes the current Android application identified as `com.spidersu.rescueteam`.

## Data the app handles

The app stores the following gameplay information **locally on the device**:

- completed missions and best move counts;
- an unfinished-mission checkpoint and undo-related game state;
- music and sound preferences; and
- an optional selected cosmetic crew insignia.

This information is used only to provide game progress and settings. It is not sent to the developer by the app.

## Data the app does not collect

The current version does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, in-app purchases, cloud saves, social features, a backend service or internet permission. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information through the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the player clears the app's storage or uninstalls the app, subject to the device/platform's normal backup and restore behavior. The developer does not receive a copy through the app and cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

## Children

Because the app does not collect personal information through the app, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If a future version changes data practices, this policy and the applicable store declarations will be updated before that version is released.
